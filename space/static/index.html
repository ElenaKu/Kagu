What is Kagu?

Kagu is a free online vectorizer. It converts raster images (PNG, JPG) into vector graphics (SVG) by automatic image tracing: flat colour areas, outlines and details become clean shapes, grouped by colour and layered, so the SVG is easy to recolour and edit in Adobe Illustrator, Inkscape, Affinity Designer or Figma. It is made for illustrations, clipart, line art and AI-generated pictures. Small pictures are upscaled x4 with Real-ESRGAN before tracing for smoother curves.
