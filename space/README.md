# Kagu — free PNG to SVG vectorizer

Kagu converts raster images (PNG, JPG) into clean, editable vector graphics (SVG) by automatic
image tracing. Upload a picture and get an SVG — no settings.

- **Image to SVG:** flat colour areas, outlines and details become clean vector shapes.
- **Editable result:** shapes are layered and grouped by colour, easy to recolour and edit in Adobe
  Illustrator, Inkscape, Affinity Designer or Figma.
- **Made for illustrations:** clipart, line art, flat and AI-generated pictures.
- **Upscaling built in:** small pictures are enlarged x4 with an AI model for digital art before tracing for smoother curves.
- **Compare before download:** the original and the SVG side by side with synced zoom.
